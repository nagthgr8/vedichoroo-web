What are Vedic Yogas?

In Vedic astrology, a yoga (Sanskrit: योग) is a specific combination of planetary positions that creates a pronounced effect in the native's life. Unlike Western astrology's aspect system, yogas are holistic patterns — they consider house lordship, sign placement, mutual aspects, and the overall strength of the planets involved.

Categories of Yogas We Detect

VedicHoroo groups its yoga analysis into eight classical categories: Sun-based yogas (Veshi, Voshi, Ubhayachari), the celebrated Pancha Mahapurusha yogas (Ruchaka, Bhadra, Hamsa, Malavya, Shasha), Moon-based yogas including the famous Gajkesari and Kemadruma, Sankhya yogas based on sign occupation, Aashraya and Dala yogas based on sign gender and house type, Akriti shape yogas, powerful Raja and Dhana yogas including seven Maha Yoga Parivartana combinations, and Combination yogas like Vajra, Kaahala, Shankha and Bheri.
